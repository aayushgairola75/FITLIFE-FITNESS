Time Management for Students

A 1‑page system so fitness helps (not hurts) your grades.

1. Plan weekly, execute daily

  • Sunday 20m: choose training days, pick top‑3 study tasks for each day.
  • Daily 5m: adjust plan; if short on time, cut volume, not the session.

2. Pomodoros that fit the gym

  • Study 50/10 blocks; gym uses similar focus intervals between sets.
  • Stack habits: review flashcards during warm‑up bike or rest periods (if safe).

3. Energy management

  • Place compound lifts when your alertness is highest.
  • Caffeine cut‑off: 7–8h before bedtime.
