Balance Life: Study • Sleep • Train • Social

A simple framework to keep fitness sustainable while you’re a student.

4-Block Day

Split your day into four blocks: Focus study (90–120m), Classes/errands, Training (45–75m incl. warm-up), and Recovery/social. Protect your sleep lane: 7–9h.

  • Keep training and study blocks at the same time most days.
  • Use a 10–15m buffer to transition and prep.
Weekly Balance

Aim for 3–4 gym days + 2 light movement days (walk, mobility). Before exams, shift to maintenance: 2 full‑body sessions/week.

Tip If you must skip, skip accessories—keep compounds.

Non‑negotiables checklist

  • Sleep 7–9h • Protein 1.6–2.2g/kg • Steps 6–10k • Water 2–3L
  • Plan your workouts on the same days you have fewer classes.
  • Batch‑cook two proteins + one carb base on Sunday.
