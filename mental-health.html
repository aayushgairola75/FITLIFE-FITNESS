Mental Health & Motivation

Simple practices to stay consistent and kind to yourself.

Motivation → Consistency
  • Attach workouts to a trigger (class ends → gym).
  • Rate your day 1–5; anything ≥2 still “counts”.
  • Keep a tiny win board: 3 checkmarks/week minimum.
Low‑mood plan
  • Swap heavy day for 20–30m walk + mobility.
  • Message a gym buddy; show up together.
  • Breathing: 4‑7‑8 x 3 rounds before sleep.
