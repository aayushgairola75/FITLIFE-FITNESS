Mind‑muscle connection • Progressive overload • Correct form • Injury prevention • Overtraining

Mind‑muscle connection

Slow the eccentric (lowering) for 2–3 seconds, pause briefly, then drive the target muscle. Use warm‑up sets to “find” the muscle before loading.

Progressive overload

Progress by one variable at a time: reps → load → sets. Example ladder: 3×8 @ 30kg → 3×10 → 3×12 → 3×8 @ 32.5kg.

Correct form (top 5 lifts)

  • Squat: Brace 360°, knees track toes, depth just below parallel. Cue: “Sit between your heels.”
  • Deadlift: Hips back, lats tight, bar close; push the floor away.
  • Bench: Shoulder blades tucked, slight arch, forearms vertical at the bottom.
  • Overhead press: Squeeze glutes and abs; move your head through at the top.
  • Row: Pull elbows toward hips; avoid shrugging.

Injury prevention

  • 10‑minute warm‑up (pulse raise → dynamic mobility → 2 light sets).
  • Respect pain; swap the movement if sharp pain persists.
  • Progress volume ≤10% per week.

Overtraining signs

  • Resting HR ↑ 5–10 bpm several days, sleep worsens, lifts dropping.
  • Fix: deload week (reduce load 10–20% and sets 30–50%).
