Study/Workout Schedule Tips

Plug‑and‑play templates you can copy to Google Calendar/Notion.

Templates

  1. 3‑Day Full Body (Mon/Wed/Fri): 60–75m sessions, Tue/Thu light cardio or mobility.
  2. Upper/Lower x4 (Mon Tue Thu Fri): Good if you have shorter daily windows.
  3. Exam Week: Full‑body A/B twice weekly (45–55m), focus on technique and sleep.

How to place sessions

  • Schedule hard study ≥3h away from hard training.
  • Use a standing timeslot (e.g., 5:30–6:45pm) and guard it like a class.
  • Pack gym bag the night before; write your first exercise and warm‑up on a sticky note.
